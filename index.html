Welcome to kWen’s Cookbook

        

          This cookbook was created to keep family recipes, comfort food favourites, and kitchen experiments intact
          for the people who matter most. It is a place for the dishes worth remembering — the ones made for family,
          shared with friends, and passed around the table.
        

        

          

            Built by kWen, this collection blends New Brunswick comfort food, handmade pasta projects,
            cozy stews, classic desserts, seafood, macarons, and the recipes that deserve to live somewhere
            better than scattered notes and half-remembered measurements.
