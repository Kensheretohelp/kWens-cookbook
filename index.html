Baking

Moist Banana Bread

Basic, reliable loaf with sour cream or plain yogurt for moisture. Makes 1 loaf.

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/3 cup sour cream or plain yogurt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Optional: 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • Optional: 3/4 cup walnuts or chocolate chips

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F / 175°C. Grease or line a standard loaf pan.
  2. Mash bananas in a large bowl, then stir in melted butter.
  3. Mix in brown sugar, eggs, sour cream or yogurt, and vanilla.
  4. Whisk flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon if using.
  5. Fold dry ingredients into wet ingredients just until combined. Do not overmix.
  6. Fold in walnuts or chocolate chips if using.
  7. Bake 55–65 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.
  8. Cool 10–15 minutes in the pan, then transfer to a rack before slicing.

kWen note: Sour cream gives a richer loaf; yogurt gives a slightly tangier, lighter one.

Pasta

Egg-Heavy Ravioli Dough

Extra-rich yolk-forward dough for ravioli. Updated locked-in 250 g flour version.

Ingredients

  • 250 g 00 flour
  • 2 whole eggs
  • 5 egg yolks
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Mound flour and make a deep well.
  2. Add whole eggs, yolks, olive oil, and salt.
  3. Beat eggs gently with a fork, gradually incorporating flour until a shaggy dough forms.
  4. Knead 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic. It may feel firm at first.
  5. Wrap tightly and rest at least 1 hour at room temperature.
  6. Roll thin, around Marcato/Atlas setting 6–7, for ravioli.

kWen note: If stiff, knead with lightly damp hands instead of adding water. If too soft, rest in the fridge briefly.

Pasta

Beef & Pork Ravioli Filling

Half-batch raw filling matched to one 250 g ravioli dough batch. Freezer-safe.

Ingredients

  • 125 g ground beef
  • 125 g ground pork
  • 25 g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1/2 egg, beaten
  • 1–2 cloves garlic, about 5 g, minced
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • Optional: pinch nutmeg or Italian seasoning
  • Optional breadcrumbs only if mixture feels loose

Method

  1. Mix filling ingredients gently until evenly combined.
  2. Keep chilled while rolling pasta.
  3. Pipe or spoon small portions onto sheets.
  4. Seal ravioli well and cook from fresh or freeze in a single layer.

kWen note: Official meat ravioli filling. Designed to match one batch of the 250 g flour ravioli dough and yield about 20–24 ravioli.

Pasta

Classic Ricotta–Spinach Ravioli Filling

Classic filling. Yields about 20–24 ravioli.

Ingredients

  • 250 g ricotta
  • 80–100 g cooked spinach, well squeezed
  • 50 g parmesan
  • 1 egg yolk, about 18 g
  • 3 g garlic, finely grated or minced
  • Pinch nutmeg
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Squeeze spinach very dry, then chop finely.
  2. Mix ricotta, spinach, parmesan, egg yolk, garlic, nutmeg, salt, and pepper.
  3. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  4. Pipe or spoon into ravioli sheets, then seal well.

kWen note: A food processor is optional, but pulse lightly only. You want smooth-ish, not gluey.

Beef Stroganoff with Striploin & No Yolks Noodles

Updated locked-in version with striploin, mushrooms, sour cream, and No Yolks noodles.

Ingredients

  • 450–600 g striploin steak, sliced into strips
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 2 tbsp butter, divided
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 225 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 small onion, sliced or diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1–2 tbsp flour, as needed for thickening
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 bag No Yolks noodles

Method

  1. Season steak strips with salt and pepper.
  2. Sear steak quickly in oil and a little butter until browned but not fully cooked through. Remove and reserve.
  3. Add remaining butter, mushrooms, and onion. Cook until softened and mushrooms release moisture.
  4. Add garlic and cook 30–60 seconds.
  5. Sprinkle in flour and stir to coat the vegetables.
  6. Add beef broth, Dijon, and Worcestershire. Simmer until the sauce coats a spoon and closes slowly when dragged through.
  7. Reduce heat or remove from heat briefly, then stir in sour cream so it does not split.
  8. Return steak and juices to the sauce just to finish warming through.
  9. Cook No Yolks noodles according to package directions and serve with stroganoff over top.

kWen note: The sauce should be noticeably thicker before sour cream goes in. If it still looks watery, reduce 2–4 minutes more.

kWen’s Classic Chicken Stew

Whole chicken stew with potatoes, optional carrots, herbes salées, rosemary, summer savory, and rolled dough boys.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, about 4–5 lb / 1.8–2.3 kg
  • 10–12 cups water
  • 1 large onion, peeled and halved
  • 5–6 medium potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 2–3 carrots, sliced, optional
  • 1 tbsp herbes salées
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary
  • 1 tbsp summer savory
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Dough boys: 2 cups / 250 g flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • About 3/4 cup / 180 ml water, enough for a soft dough

Method

  1. Place whole chicken in a large stockpot and cover with water. Add onion, rosemary, summer savory, and herbes salées.
  2. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce heat and simmer 1 1/2–2 hours, skimming foam as needed, until chicken is tender.
  3. Remove chicken and cool slightly. Discard onion.
  4. Pull chicken from bones and return meat to the pot. Discard skin and bones.
  5. Add carrots and simmer 5–10 minutes.
  6. While carrots simmer, mix flour and salt for the dough. Add olive oil and enough water to form a smooth dough.
  7. Knead briefly, roll out on a lightly floured surface, and cut into small squares or rectangles.
  8. Add cubed potatoes to the stew.
  9. Shortly after adding potatoes, drop in the dough pieces.
  10. Simmer until potatoes are fork tender and dough is cooked through, about 10–15 minutes.
  11. Taste and adjust salt and pepper before serving.

kWen note: No celery and no baking powder. The dough and cubed potatoes should finish cooking at roughly the same time.

kWen’s Cipâte

Long-cooked French Canadian layered meat pie with meatballs, cubed meats, potatoes, onions, broth, and rolled oil dough.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb / 450 g small meatballs or ground beef, formed about 1 inch or smaller
  • 1 lb / 450 g cubed beef
  • 1 lb / 450 g cubed chicken
  • 1 lb / 450 g cubed veal or lamb
  • 5–6 medium potatoes, peeled and diced small
  • 2 large onions, sliced
  • Salt and black pepper, liberally
  • 1 tbsp summer savory
  • 1 tbsp parsley
  • Dough: 5 cups / 625 g flour
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup / 120 ml oil
  • About 1 1/2 cups / 360 ml water, enough for a soft dough
  • 6–10 cups chicken broth, plus more as needed through cooking

Method

  1. Mix flour and salt for the dough. Add oil and enough water to form a smooth workable dough. Knead briefly and divide into portions.
  2. Season meats generously with salt, pepper, summer savory, and parsley.
  3. Roll dough portions into thin sheets for layering.
  4. In a large deep roasting pan or heavy Dutch oven, layer meat, potatoes, sliced onions, and a sheet of dough.
  5. Repeat the layers until the dish is full.
  6. Pour chicken broth over the layers until very well moistened but not overflowing.
  7. Cover tightly and cook hot for the first hour, around 400°F / 205°C.
  8. Turn oven off overnight and leave the cipâte inside.
  9. The next morning, turn oven very low, around 225–250°F / 110–120°C, and cook another 10–12 hours at a gentle simmer.
  10. Add chicken broth repeatedly through the day so it stays moist and never dries out.

kWen note: The long cook is the heart of the recipe. Dough softens into tender layers and potatoes absorb the meat juices and broth.

Braised Beef Pot Pie

Chuck or blade roast braised until tender in rich gravy, finished under flaky pie crust or puff pastry.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 lb / 1.1 kg chuck roast, blade roast, or stewing beef, cut into chunks
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 3 carrots, sliced
  • 225 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 1 cup red wine or extra broth
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp rosemary
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Pie dough or puff pastry for topping

Method

  1. Pat beef dry, season generously, and toss lightly with flour.
  2. Brown beef deeply in batches in a Dutch oven with oil. Remove and set aside.
  3. Cook onion, carrots, and mushrooms in the same pot until softened and mushrooms release moisture.
  4. Add garlic and tomato paste and cook 1–2 minutes.
  5. Deglaze with red wine or broth, scraping up browned bits.
  6. Return beef to the pot. Add beef broth, Worcestershire, thyme, rosemary, and bay leaf.
  7. Cover and braise at 325°F / 163°C for about 3 hours, until fork tender.
  8. Remove lid for the last 20–30 minutes if needed to thicken. Remove bay leaf.
  9. Cool filling slightly, transfer to a pie dish, and top with pie crust or puff pastry.
  10. Bake at 400°F / 205°C for 40–50 minutes, until golden and bubbling.

kWen note: Freezer-friendly. Best frozen as filling, then topped with fresh pastry before baking.

kWen’s Guinness Beef Stew with Irish Cheddar Mash

Dark, rich Guinness beef stew ladled over creamy cheddar mashed potatoes.

Ingredients

  • Stew: 3 lb / 1.4 kg chuck roast, blade roast, or stewing beef, cut into chunks
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 large onions, diced
  • 3 carrots, sliced
  • 225 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 can Guinness stout, 440–500 ml
  • 3 cups / 720 ml beef broth
  • Mash: 3 lb / 1.4 kg Yukon Gold potatoes
  • 4 tbsp / 56 g butter
  • 1/2–3/4 cup / 120–180 ml milk or cream
  • 2 cups / 200 g sharp Irish cheddar, shredded
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Pat beef dry, season generously, and toss lightly with flour.
  2. Brown beef deeply in batches in a Dutch oven. Remove and reserve.
  3. Cook onions 5–7 minutes, then add carrots and mushrooms. Cook until mushrooms release moisture.
  4. Add garlic, tomato paste, Worcestershire, and thyme. Cook 1–2 minutes.
  5. Pour in Guinness and scrape up browned bits. Simmer about 5 minutes to mellow bitterness.
  6. Return beef to the pot. Add beef broth and bay leaf.
  7. Cover and braise at 325°F / 163°C for 3–3 1/2 hours, until beef is very tender.
  8. Remove lid during the final 20–30 minutes if needed to thicken. Remove bay leaf and adjust seasoning.
  9. For mash, boil potatoes in salted water until fork tender. Drain well.
  10. Mash with butter and milk or cream, then stir in cheddar and green onions. Season to taste.
  11. Serve stew ladled generously over cheddar mash.

kWen note: No cabbage in this version. Guinness should add roasted depth without tasting harsh.

Italian Wedding Soup

Frozen meatballs and orzo version.

Ingredients

  • Olive oil
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 celery stalk
  • Garlic
  • 8 cups / 2 L chicken broth
  • 500 g frozen fully cooked Italian meatballs
  • 150 g / 3/4 cup orzo
  • 2 cups spinach
  • Oregano
  • Thyme
  • Parmesan
  • Optional lemon

Method

  1. Sauté onion, carrot, celery, and garlic in olive oil.
  2. Add chicken broth and bring to a simmer.
  3. Add frozen meatballs and simmer until heated through.
  4. Add orzo and cook 8–10 minutes.
  5. Stir in spinach until wilted.
  6. Finish with parmesan and optional lemon.

kWen note: Optional egg drizzle variation can be added at the end.

Hamburger Stew

NB classic with ground beef, potatoes, carrots, onion, tomato, and broth.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb / 450 g ground beef
  • 4 medium potatoes, diced
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 can / 28 oz diced tomatoes or 2 cups chopped tomato
  • 4 cups beef or chicken broth
  • 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • Optional: 1 tsp savory, parsley, or Italian herbs

Method

  1. Brown ground beef and drain excess fat if needed.
  2. Add onion and cook until softened.
  3. Add potatoes, carrots, tomato, and broth.
  4. Simmer until vegetables are tender.
  5. Season to taste and serve hot.

kWen note: This is the stew-style version kept in the cookbook.

Tourtière

Acadian/Brayon-style meat pie.

Ingredients

  • Ground pork and/or beef
  • Onion
  • Potato water or broth for binding
  • Summer savory
  • Salt and pepper
  • Pie crust
  • Egg wash

Method

  1. Cook meat with onion and seasoning until tender and savory.
  2. Use potato water or broth to bind the filling.
  3. Cool filling slightly.
  4. Fill pie crust, top with second crust, vent, and brush with egg wash.
  5. Bake until crust is golden and filling is hot.

kWen note: Family-style savory meat pie; potato water can be used instead of broth.

Garlic Soy Butter Salmon

Apple cider vinegar version with updated cook times.

Ingredients

  • 2–3 salmon fillets, 150–180 g each
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 2 tbsp / 28 g butter
  • 1 1/2 tbsp / 24 g soy sauce or tamari
  • 3/4 tsp / 3.75 g apple cider vinegar
  • 1 small garlic clove, about 3 g
  • Optional chili flakes or black pepper

Method

  1. Season salmon lightly.
  2. For pan-seared: heat oil over medium and sear skin-side down 7–8 minutes.
  3. Flip, lower heat, add butter, soy, vinegar, and garlic.
  4. Baste 5–6 minutes until just cooked.
  5. For oven-baked: bake skin-side down at 400°F / 205°C for 15–17 minutes, spooning sauce over before baking. Optional broil 1–2 minutes.

kWen note: Keep heat moderate because soy and butter can scorch. If sharp, add 1/2 tsp honey or maple.

Sugar Pie / Tarte au Sucre

Canadian sugar pie with maple and cream. Makes one 9-inch pie.

Ingredients

  • 210 g brown sugar
  • 40 g all-purpose flour
  • 30 ml cornstarch
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 375 ml / 1 1/2 cups 35% whipping cream
  • 125 ml / 1/2 cup maple syrup
  • 2 pie crusts / pâte brisée
  • Milk for brushing

Method

  1. In a saucepan off heat, whisk brown sugar, flour, cornstarch, and salt.
  2. Add cream and maple syrup; mix well.
  3. Bring to a boil while stirring, then remove from heat and cool slightly.
  4. Line a 23 cm / 9 in pie plate with one crust and pour in filling.
  5. Brush edges with milk.
  6. Cut second crust into 2.5 cm / 1 in strips, brush with milk, and weave lattice over filling.
  7. Trim and seal edges.
  8. Bake at 400°F / 200°C on the low rack for 35–40 minutes until golden and bubbling.

kWen note: Serve warm or cold.

Mom’s Texas Brownies

Family sheet-pan brownie/cake classic.

Ingredients

  • Cake: 2 cups flour
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup shortening
  • 1 cup coffee
  • 1/4 cup cocoa
  • 1/2 cup buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Frosting: 1/2 cup butter
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 3 1/2 cups powdered sugar
  • 1 tbsp vanilla

Method

  1. Prepare a 17.5 x 11 inch sheet pan.
  2. Make cake batter with flour, sugar, butter, shortening, coffee, cocoa, buttermilk, eggs, baking soda, and vanilla.
  3. Bake until set.
  4. For frosting, heat butter, cocoa, and milk.
  5. Beat in powdered sugar and vanilla.
  6. Spread frosting over warm cake.

kWen note: This is the only cookbook recipe planned to include handwritten recipe imagery as a subtle background design element.

Classic Tiramisu

Classic layered coffee dessert.

Ingredients

  • 24 ladyfingers
  • 1 1/2 cups strong coffee or espresso, cooled
  • 475 g mascarpone
  • 4 large egg yolks or 1 1/2 cups whipped cream method
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder for dusting

Method

  1. Prepare coffee and let cool.
  2. Make mascarpone cream.
  3. Dip ladyfingers briefly in coffee.
  4. Layer ladyfingers and cream.
  5. Chill until set.
  6. Dust with cocoa before serving.

kWen note: Best after a long chill so the layers settle together.
