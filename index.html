Baking

Moist Banana Bread

Basic, reliable loaf with sour cream or plain yogurt for moisture. Makes 1 loaf.

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/3 cup sour cream or plain yogurt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Optional: 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • Optional: 3/4 cup walnuts or chocolate chips

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F / 175°C. Grease or line a standard loaf pan.
  2. Mash bananas in a large bowl. Stir in melted butter.
  3. Mix in brown sugar, eggs, sour cream or yogurt, and vanilla.
  4. Whisk flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon if using.
  5. Fold dry ingredients into wet ingredients just until combined. Do not overmix.
  6. Fold in walnuts or chocolate chips if using.
  7. Bake 55–65 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.
  8. Cool 10–15 minutes in the pan, then transfer to a rack before slicing.

kWen note: Sour cream gives a richer loaf; yogurt gives a slightly tangier, lighter one.

Pasta

Egg-Heavy Ravioli Dough

Extra-rich yolk-forward dough for ravioli.

Ingredients

  • 250 g 00 flour
  • 2 whole eggs
  • 5 egg yolks
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Mound flour and make a deep well.
  2. Add whole eggs, yolks, olive oil, and salt.
  3. Beat eggs gently with a fork, gradually incorporating flour until a shaggy dough forms.
  4. Knead 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic. It may feel firm at first.
  5. Wrap tightly and rest at least 1 hour at room temperature.
  6. Roll thin, around Marcato/Atlas setting 6–7, for ravioli.

kWen note: If stiff, knead with lightly damp hands instead of adding water. If too soft, rest in the fridge briefly.

Pasta

Classic Ricotta–Spinach Ravioli Filling

Yields about 20–24 ravioli.

Ingredients

  • 250 g ricotta
  • 80–100 g cooked spinach, well squeezed
  • 50 g parmesan
  • 1 egg yolk, about 18 g
  • 3 g garlic, finely grated or minced
  • Pinch nutmeg
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Squeeze spinach very dry, then chop finely.
  2. Mix ricotta, spinach, parmesan, egg yolk, garlic, nutmeg, salt, and pepper.
  3. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  4. Pipe or spoon into ravioli sheets, then seal well.

kWen note: A food processor is optional, but pulse lightly only. You want smooth-ish, not gluey.

Pizza

Slow-Rise Thin Crust Pizza Dough

KitchenAid mixer method, 4–5 hour rise, makes 2 pizzas.

Ingredients

  • 300 g 00 flour or all-purpose flour
  • 195 g water, room temperature
  • 1 tsp instant yeast or active dry yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Mix ingredients in a stand mixer until a smooth dough forms.
  2. Knead until elastic.
  3. Cover and rise 4–5 hours.
  4. Divide into 2 dough balls.
  5. Stretch thin and top as desired.
  6. Bake hot until crisp and browned.

kWen note: Thin, crisp crust style. Works well when stretched for 12-inch pizzas.

Breakfast

Best Buttermilk Pancakes

Richer, more custardy buttermilk pancake style.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups / 190 g all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 1/4 cups / 300 ml buttermilk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 tbsp / 42 g melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Optional: 3/4 cup wild blueberries

Method

  1. Whisk dry ingredients.
  2. Whisk buttermilk, eggs, melted butter, and vanilla.
  3. Combine gently; leave some small lumps.
  4. Rest batter briefly.
  5. Cook on a buttered griddle until golden on both sides.
  6. Add blueberries directly to each pancake if using.

kWen note: Goal is richer/custardy rather than diner-style fluffy-dry.

Sugar Pie / Tarte au Sucre

Canadian sugar pie with maple and cream. Makes one 9-inch pie.

Ingredients

  • 210 g brown sugar
  • 40 g all-purpose flour
  • 30 ml cornstarch
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 375 ml / 1 1/2 cups 35% whipping cream
  • 125 ml / 1/2 cup maple syrup
  • 2 pie crusts / pâte brisée
  • Milk for brushing

Method

  1. In a saucepan off heat, whisk brown sugar, flour, cornstarch, and salt.
  2. Add cream and maple syrup; mix well.
  3. Bring to a boil while stirring, then remove from heat and cool slightly.
  4. Line a 23 cm / 9 in pie plate with one crust and pour in filling.
  5. Brush edges with milk.
  6. Cut second crust into 2.5 cm / 1 in strips, brush with milk, and weave lattice over filling.
  7. Trim and seal edges.
  8. Bake at 400°F / 200°C on the low rack for 35–40 minutes until golden and bubbling.

kWen note: Serve warm or cold.

Dessert

Old-Fashioned Graham Pie

New Brunswick style graham pie.

Ingredients

  • 1 graham cracker crust
  • 1/2 cup / 100 g brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp / 8 g all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp / 16 g cornstarch
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups / 360 ml heavy cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F / 175°C.
  2. In a saucepan, whisk brown sugar, flour, and cornstarch.
  3. Gradually add heavy cream, stirring constantly until thickened.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in eggs and vanilla.
  5. Pour into graham cracker crust.
  6. Bake 30–35 minutes, or until set.
  7. Cool before serving.

kWen note: Classic creamy brown sugar filling in a graham crust.

Uses 100 g egg whites in shells; no coconut cream in filling.

Seafood

Garlic Soy Butter Salmon

Apple cider vinegar version. Serves 2–3.

Ingredients

  • 2–3 salmon fillets, 150–180 g / 5–6 oz each
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 tbsp / 14 g neutral oil
  • Sauce: 2 tbsp / 28 g butter
  • 1 1/2 tbsp / 24 g soy sauce or tamari
  • 3/4 tsp / 3.75 g apple cider vinegar
  • 1 small garlic clove / about 3 g, finely minced or grated
  • Optional chili flakes or black pepper

Method

  1. Season salmon lightly.
  2. For pan-seared: heat oil over medium and sear skin-side down 7–8 minutes.
  3. Flip, lower heat, and add butter. Stir in soy, vinegar, and garlic.
  4. Baste 5–6 minutes until just cooked.
  5. For oven-baked: heat oven to 400°F / 205°C. Place salmon skin-side down, season, spoon sauce over, and bake 15–17 minutes. Optional broil 1–2 minutes.

kWen note: Keep heat moderate; soy and butter can scorch. Target about 125–130°F / 52–54°C internal.

Soup

Chicken Rice Soup

Ken’s classic version with Minute Rice.

Ingredients

  • 3 L / 12 cups chicken broth
  • 2 chicken breasts or equivalent cooked/raw chicken
  • 3 celery stalks, sliced
  • 1 large carrot, shredded
  • 3 green onions, chopped and divided
  • 1 tbsp parsley
  • 1 cup Minute Rice
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Bring chicken broth to a gentle boil.
  2. Add celery and boil about 5 minutes.
  3. Add shredded carrot, parsley, and half the green onions. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Add chicken, cubed or small pieces. Simmer until cooked and tender, about 10–15 minutes raw or 5 minutes cooked.
  5. Stir in Minute Rice and simmer 5–10 minutes until soft and slightly thickened.
  6. Stir in remaining green onions just before serving.

kWen note: Works beautifully with leftover or rotisserie chicken. Add a pinch of bouillon for stronger broth if desired.

Dad’s Chicken Stew with Dough Boys

Whole chicken stew with herbes salées, rosemary, summer savory, potatoes, carrots, and dough boys.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken
  • 8 medium potatoes, extra preferred
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 1 onion, boiled with chicken then removed
  • 1 tbsp herbes salées
  • 1/2 tsp rosemary
  • 1 tbsp summer savory
  • Dough boys: 2 cups flour
  • 3/4 cup water, as needed
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Boil whole chicken with onion until cooked and flavorful.

  6. Mix dough boys from flour, water, salt, and olive oil.
  7. Drop dough into simmering stew and cook until done.

kWen note: Few carrots, extra potatoes — that is the house style.

No overnight drying; timed to pair with roasted potatoes.

  • 1 whole chicken
  • 1 tbsp salt, or to taste
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp oil or butter if desired
  • 1–2 tbsp preferred seasonings
  • Potatoes for separate roasting

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.

Basics

Crisco Pie Dough

4-crust version, no egg, no vinegar, KitchenAid mixer method.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups / 315 g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp / 6 g salt
  • 1 cup / 200 g Crisco shortening
  • 1/4 cup / 60 ml cold water

Method

  1. In KitchenAid bowl, combine flour and salt.
  2. Using paddle attachment, cut in Crisco until mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  3. Gradually add cold water, mixing on low until dough begins to form.
  4. Divide into 4 portions.
  5. Roll each portion to fit pie dish.

kWen note: Use as directed for pie recipes.
